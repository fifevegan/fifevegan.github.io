How to Make Vegan South African Chakalaka

Spicy, curried vegetable relish — brilliant with pap or bread.

South AfricanAfrican12 ingredients6 stepsAllergens: gluten

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 1 red and 1 green pepper, diced
  • 2 chillies, sliced (to taste)
  • 400 g tin baked beans in tomato sauce
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt
  • Pap or crusty bread, to serve

Method

  1. Fry the onion in the oil for 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the curry powder and ginger; cook 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the carrots, peppers, and chillies; cook 5 minutes until beginning to soften.
  4. Add the tomatoes and sugar; simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Stir in the baked beans and simmer 5 more minutes until thick.
  6. Season and serve warm or at room temperature with pap or bread.
