How to Make Vegan Trinidadian Doubles

Port of Spain's favourite street breakfast: soft fried bara flatbreads filled with curried channa and pepper sauce.

TrinidadianCaribbean10 ingredients4 stepsAllergens: gluten

Ingredients

  • For the bara: 250 g plain flour, 1 tsp instant yeast, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp sugar, oil for frying
  • For the channa: 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp green seasoning (or chopped coriander, thyme, and spring onion)
  • Scotch bonnet pepper sauce, to serve
  • Salt

Method

  1. Mix the bara flour, yeast, turmeric, sugar, and a pinch of salt with warm water into a soft dough; rest 1 hour until puffy.
  2. For the channa, fry the onion and garlic in oil, add curry and cumin with a splash of water, then the chickpeas and green seasoning; simmer 15 minutes, lightly crushing some chickpeas to thicken.
  3. Pinch off balls of dough, stretch into thin rounds, and fry 30–40 seconds per side until golden and puffed.
  4. Sandwich a spoonful of curried channa between two bara, add pepper sauce, and eat hot.
