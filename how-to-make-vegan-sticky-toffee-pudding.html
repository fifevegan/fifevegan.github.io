How to Make Vegan Sticky Toffee Pudding

Dark date sponge drowned in hot toffee sauce.

BritishWestern10 ingredients5 stepsAllergens: gluten

Ingredients

  • 200 g dates, pitted and chopped
  • 250 ml boiling water
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 75 g dairy-free butter
  • 150 g dark brown sugar
  • 200 g self-raising flour
  • 150 ml plant milk
  • 100 g golden syrup or maple syrup
  • 100 ml plant cream
  • Custard or vegan ice cream, to serve

Method

  1. Soak the dates in the boiling water with the bicarb for 15 minutes, then mash roughly.
  2. Cream the butter and half the sugar; fold in the flour, plant milk, and the date mixture.
  3. Bake in a lined tin at 180 C (fan 160 C) for 30-35 minutes until springy.
  4. Simmer the remaining sugar, syrup, and plant cream for 5 minutes to make the toffee sauce.
  5. Serve warm squares of sponge drowned in the hot sauce with custard.
