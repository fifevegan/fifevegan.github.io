How to Make Vegan Jerk Jackfruit

Pulled jackfruit in fiery jerk marinade, charred and served with lime and rice.

JamaicanCaribbean11 ingredients6 steps

Ingredients

  • 2 tins (400 g each) young green jackfruit in brine, drained and rinsed
  • 2 tbsp jerk seasoning (allspice, thyme, Scotch bonnet, cinnamon, nutmeg)
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce (or tamari)
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 100 ml vegetable stock
  • Salt

Method

  1. Shred the jackfruit with two forks, discarding any hard core pieces.
  2. Toss with jerk seasoning, soy sauce, sugar and half the lime juice; marinate 20 minutes.
  3. Sear the jackfruit in hot oil for 6-8 minutes until edges char.
  4. Add onion and garlic; cook 4 minutes until soft.
  5. Stir in tomato purée and stock; simmer 10 minutes until thick and glossy.
  6. Finish with remaining lime juice and serve with rice & peas.
