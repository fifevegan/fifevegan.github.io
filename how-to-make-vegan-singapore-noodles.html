How to Make Vegan Singapore Noodles

Golden curry-spiced rice vermicelli with crisp vegetables.

ChineseAsian10 ingredients5 stepsAllergens: gluten, sesame, soy

Ingredients

  • 250 g rice vermicelli
  • 200 g firm tofu, thinly sliced
  • 1 red pepper, thinly sliced
  • 2 carrots, julienned
  • 100 g beansprouts
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

Method

  1. Soak the vermicelli in hot water for 5 minutes; drain well.
  2. Fry the tofu in the vegetable oil until golden; set aside.
  3. Stir-fry the pepper and carrot for 2 minutes; add the curry powder and cook 30 seconds.
  4. Add the noodles, soy sauce, and a splash of water; toss for 2 minutes until evenly golden.
  5. Return the tofu, add the beansprouts and spring onions, toss once more, and serve.
