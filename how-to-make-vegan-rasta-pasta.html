How to Make Vegan Rasta Pasta

Creamy jerk-spiced pasta with bell peppers in a coconut sauce — Jamaican-Italian fusion.

JamaicanCaribbean12 ingredients6 stepsAllergens: gluten

Ingredients

  • 400 g penne or rigatoni
  • 3 bell peppers (red, yellow, green), sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp jerk seasoning
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 100 g vegan cream cheese (optional; choose soy-free if needed)
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1/2 Scotch bonnet, deseeded and minced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh thyme

Method

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water until al dente; reserve 150 ml pasta water.
  2. Sauté peppers and onion in oil for 5 minutes until just soft.
  3. Add garlic, Scotch bonnet, thyme and jerk seasoning; cook 1 minute.
  4. Pour in coconut milk and simmer 3 minutes; stir in vegan cream cheese if using.
  5. Toss in the pasta with a splash of pasta water until glossy and coated.
  6. Season, scatter spring onions over and serve immediately.
