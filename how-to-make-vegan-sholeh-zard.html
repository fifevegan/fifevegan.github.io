How to Make Vegan Sholeh Zard

Persia's golden saffron rice pudding, delicately scented with rosewater and cardamom and crowned with cinnamon and slivered almonds. A treasured festive dessert that happens to be completely plant-based.

PersianMiddle Eastern8 ingredients5 stepsAllergens: tree_nuts

Ingredients

  • 150 g pudding rice
  • 1.2 L water
  • 200 g sugar
  • ½ tsp saffron threads, bloomed in 2 tbsp hot water
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 tbsp rosewater
  • 50 g slivered almonds
  • Ground cinnamon, for decorating

Method

  1. Wash the rice, then simmer in the water for 25-30 minutes, stirring now and then, until very soft and beginning to break down.
  2. Stir in the sugar and simmer for 10 more minutes until creamy.
  3. Add the bloomed saffron, cardamom, rosewater and most of the almonds; cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Spoon into a serving bowl or individual cups.
  5. Decorate with cinnamon patterns and the remaining almonds; serve warm or chilled.
