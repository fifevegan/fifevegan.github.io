How to Make Vegan Koshari

Cairo's beloved national dish: a hearty jumble of rice, lentils, macaroni and chickpeas under a spiced tomato sauce and a crown of crispy fried onions. Comforting, budget-friendly and completely plant-based.

EgyptianMiddle Eastern12 ingredients6 stepsAllergens: gluten

Ingredients

  • 150 g brown lentils
  • 150 g white rice
  • 100 g macaroni
  • 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp tomato purée
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, plus oil for frying
  • 1 tbsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp white vinegar
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Rinse the lentils, cover with water and simmer for 15 minutes until just tender; drain.
  2. Cook the rice according to packet instructions and boil the macaroni until al dente; keep warm.
  3. Fry the sliced onions in hot oil over medium-high heat for 8-10 minutes until deep golden and crisp; drain on kitchen paper.
  4. Simmer the chopped tomatoes, tomato purée, half the garlic, cumin and vinegar for 10 minutes until slightly thickened; season well.
  5. Warm the chickpeas in a small pan with a splash of water and salt.
  6. Assemble: layer rice, lentils, macaroni and chickpeas in bowls, spoon over the tomato sauce and top generously with crispy onions.
