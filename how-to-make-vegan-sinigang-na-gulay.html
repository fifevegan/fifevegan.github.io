How to Make Vegan Sinigang na Gulay (Tamarind Vegetable Soup)

A comforting, sour Filipino soup bursting with tamarind, tender vegetables, and silken tofu. Every spoonful is bright, warming, and deeply nourishing.

FilipinoAsian12 ingredients6 stepsAllergens: soy

Ingredients

  • 1.5L vegetable stock
  • 200g okra, halved
  • 1 daikon radish, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, quartered
  • 100g string beans
  • 1 pack silken tofu, cubed
  • 3 tbsp tamarind paste (or sinigang mix)
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 green chilli, sliced
  • 1 bunch kangkong (water spinach) or spinach
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil

Method

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Sauté onion and garlic until fragrant, about 3 minutes.
  2. Add tomatoes and cook down for 5 minutes until softened. Pour in vegetable stock and bring to a gentle boil.
  3. Stir in tamarind paste, daikon, okra, and string beans. Simmer for 10 minutes until vegetables are tender.
  4. Gently add tofu cubes and green chilli. Simmer for another 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in water spinach, cook for 1 minute until wilted. Taste and adjust sourness with extra tamarind if desired.
  6. Ladle into bowls and serve steaming hot with steamed rice on the side.
