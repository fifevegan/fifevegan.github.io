How to Make Vegan Kelewele

A beloved Ghanaian street snack of ripe plantain cubes tossed in ginger, chilli and warm spices, then fried until caramelised and golden. Sweet, spicy and utterly moreish.

GhanaianAfrican8 ingredients5 steps

Ingredients

  • 2 large ripe plantains (yellow with black spots), peeled and cut into cubes
  • 1 thumb fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp ground cayenne pepper
  • ½ tsp ground aniseed or fennel seeds
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp fine salt
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Method

  1. In a large bowl, toss the plantain cubes with the ginger, garlic, cayenne, aniseed, smoked paprika and salt until evenly coated; rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Heat about 3 cm of oil in a deep pan to 170°C.
  3. Fry the plantain cubes in batches for 4-6 minutes, turning occasionally, until deep golden and caramelised at the edges.
  4. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on kitchen paper.
  5. Serve hot, sprinkled with a little extra salt, as a snack or side dish.
