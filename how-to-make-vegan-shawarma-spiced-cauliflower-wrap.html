How to Make Vegan Shawarma-Spiced Cauliflower Wrap

Charred shawarma cauliflower with garlic sauce and pickles.

LevantineMiddle Eastern9 ingredients5 stepsAllergens: gluten

Ingredients

  • 1 large cauliflower, broken into florets
  • 2 tbsp shawarma spice blend (cumin, coriander, paprika, turmeric, cinnamon)
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 flatbreads or wraps
  • 4 tbsp vegan garlic sauce or toum-style mayo
  • Shredded lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • Pickled turnips or gherkins
  • Salt

Method

  1. Toss the cauliflower with the shawarma spices, oil, and salt.
  2. Roast at 220°C (fan 200°C) for 25–30 minutes until charred at the edges and tender.
  3. Warm the flatbreads.
  4. Spread each with garlic sauce, then pile on cauliflower, lettuce, tomato, and pickles.
  5. Roll tightly, wrap in paper, and slice in half.
