Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026 · Last updated: 30 August 2026

FIFE: A Vegan Lifestyle ("FIFE", "the App", "we", "us", "our") is a mobile application and website. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have. We are the "data controller" for the personal data described here under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and, where it applies, the EU GDPR.

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hello@fife.earth.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to everyone who uses the FIFE mobile app (Android and iOS) and the FIFE website. FIFE is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16 — see Section 9.

2. The data we collect

We practise data minimisation: we only collect what the app actually needs to work.

What we don't collect: no date of birth, no precise GPS tracks, no contact lists, no photos other than ones you deliberately post, no payment card numbers (billing is handled entirely by the Apple App Store and Google Play as in-app purchases — we never see your card details), and no special-category or otherwise sensitive data. Data minimisation is a design choice, not just a policy line.

3. How we use your data

  • Provide, maintain and improve the App — UCHE, the Feed, the discovery map, recipes and shopping lists.
  • Process subscriptions and unlock Creator features.
  • Send service messages (account confirmation, security alerts, policy changes) and — only if you opted in — launch news.
  • Moderate user content to keep the community safe and lawful.
  • Analyse aggregate usage to decide what to build next.
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g. tax records, lawful requests).

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

5. International transfers

Our core database is hosted in the EU. Our product analytics are processed by PostHog, Inc. in the United States; this transfer relies on Standard Contractual Clauses with appropriate safeguards. Some other processors (RevenueCat, Google, Moonshot AI) may also process limited data outside the UK/EU, relying on adequacy decisions (including the EU-US and UK-US Data Privacy Frameworks) or Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us to delete your analytics data at any time by emailing hello@fife.earth.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data: kept while your account is active; deleted within 30 days of account deletion, unless law requires longer.
  • User content: kept until you delete it or your account; anonymised after account deletion.
  • UCHE chat history: kept for conversation continuity; deleted with your account or earlier if you clear it.
  • Analytics: retained in aggregate or de-identified form for up to 24 months.
  • Billing/tax records: kept for the statutory period (typically 6–7 years in the UK) as required by law.
  • Waitlist/newsletter: kept until you unsubscribe.

7. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time (location, newsletter, analytics) — this does not affect processing already carried out
  • Complain to a supervisory authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU, your local data protection authority

Delete your account in-app: Profile → Delete my account. For any other data request, email hello@fife.earth — we respond within one month (usually much faster during beta).

8. Cookies and tracking

The mobile app does not use browser cookies. This website uses no tracking cookies and no advertising trackers. In-app analytics (PostHog, processed in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses) collect pseudonymous usage events as described in Section 2.

9. Children

FIFE is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, contact hello@fife.earth and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, hashed passwords via Supabase Auth, and row-level access controls in our database. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the relevant authority of any breach where the law requires it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will notify you in-app (and by email if you opted in) before they take effect, and update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the revised policy.
