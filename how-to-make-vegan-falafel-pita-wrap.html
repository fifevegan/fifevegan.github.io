How to Make Vegan Falafel Pita Wrap

Herb-packed crispy falafel with tahini sauce and pickles.

LebaneseMiddle Eastern14 ingredients5 stepsAllergens: gluten, sesame

Ingredients

  • 250 g dried chickpeas, soaked overnight (never tinned)
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • Large bunch parsley and coriander
  • 1 tbsp cumin
  • 1 tbsp coriander seeds, crushed
  • ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 4 pita breads
  • 4 tbsp tahini
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • Shredded lettuce, tomato, and pickles
  • Oil for frying
  • Salt

Method

  1. Blitz the soaked chickpeas, onion, garlic, and herbs in a food processor to a coarse, sandy paste.
  2. Mix in the cumin, coriander seeds, bicarb, flour, and 1 tsp salt; rest 30 minutes.
  3. Shape into balls and deep-fry at 175°C for 3–4 minutes until deep brown and crisp.
  4. Whisk the tahini with lemon juice and enough water to make a pourable sauce; season.
  5. Stuff pitas with falafel, salad, pickles, and plenty of tahini sauce.
