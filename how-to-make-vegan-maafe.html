How to Make Vegan Maafe (West African Peanut Stew)

Velvety peanut-tomato stew with sweet potato — deeply comforting.

SenegaleseAfrican14 ingredients5 stepsAllergens: peanuts

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 150 g natural peanut butter
  • 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 500 ml vegetable stock
  • 1 sweet potato, cubed
  • 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
  • 100 g spinach
  • 1 scotch bonnet, whole (optional)
  • Rice, to serve
  • Salt

Method

  1. Soften the onion in the oil for 5 minutes; add garlic and ginger for 1 minute.
  2. Fry the tomato purée for 2 minutes, then whisk in the peanut butter until smooth.
  3. Add the tomatoes, stock, sweet potato, and scotch bonnet; simmer 20 minutes.
  4. Add the chickpeas and simmer 10 more minutes until the sweet potato is tender and the stew is thick.
  5. Fold in the spinach to wilt, remove the scotch bonnet, season, and serve over rice.
