How to Make Vegan Fresh Spring Rolls (Goi Cuon)

Rice-paper rolls bursting with herbs, noodles, and peanut dip.

VietnameseAsian8 ingredients5 stepsAllergens: gluten, peanuts, soy

Ingredients

  • 8 rice paper wrappers
  • 100 g rice vermicelli, cooked and cooled
  • 200 g firm tofu, sliced and pan-fried
  • 1 carrot and ½ cucumber, julienned
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Large handfuls of mint and coriander
  • Dip: 3 tbsp peanut butter, 1 tbsp hoisin, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sriracha, 3–4 tbsp warm water
  • Crushed peanuts

Method

  1. Whisk the dipping sauce until smooth; top with crushed peanuts.
  2. Dip a rice paper in warm water for 5 seconds and lay on a damp board.
  3. Layer lettuce, noodles, tofu, carrot, cucumber, and herbs on the lower third.
  4. Fold in the sides and roll up tightly like a burrito.
  5. Repeat, keep under a damp cloth, and serve with the peanut dip.
