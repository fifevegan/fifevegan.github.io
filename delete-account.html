Delete your FIFE account

This page explains how to delete your FIFE — A Vegan Friendly Lifestyle account and what happens to your data.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — in the app (fastest):

  1. Open FIFE and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — by email: send a request to hello@fife.earth from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete accounts requested this way within 7 days.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  1. Your profile (name, username, email address, profile photo)
  2. Your posts, comments and likes in the Feed
  3. Your saved recipes, cookbook and shopping lists
  4. Your conversation history with UCHE
  5. Your preferences and settings

What may be kept, and for how long

Security and backup logs may retain minimal technical records (such as an account identifier in encrypted backups) for up to 30 days, after which they are permanently erased. We keep nothing else — no email address, no content, no activity history.
