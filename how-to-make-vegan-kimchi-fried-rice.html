How to Make Vegan Kimchi Fried Rice

Fiery, funky fried rice with crispy edges and sesame.

KoreanAsian11 ingredients5 stepsAllergens: gluten, sesame, soy

Ingredients

  • 400 g cooked rice, ideally a day old
  • 200 g vegan kimchi, chopped, plus 2 tbsp kimchi juice
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • Nori strips (optional)

Method

  1. Fry the onion in the neutral oil for 4 minutes; add garlic and kimchi and cook 3 minutes until the kimchi caramelises slightly.
  2. Stir in the gochujang, kimchi juice, and soy sauce.
  3. Add the rice, breaking up clumps, and fry for 5 minutes until hot and slightly crispy at the edges.
  4. Drizzle over the sesame oil and toss.
  5. Serve topped with spring onions, sesame seeds, and nori strips.
