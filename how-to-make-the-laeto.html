How to Make Vegan The Laeto

Lagos's premiere noodle-style meal and the fan's favourite 🔥 — a brothy, fiery noodle pot: pepper-soup spices meet springy noodles.

NigerianAfrican11 ingredients6 stepsAllergens: gluten, soy

Ingredients

  • 2 packs instant noodles or 200 g wheat noodles
  • 1 litre vegetable stock
  • 1 tbsp pepper-soup spice mix (or 1 tsp each ground ginger, garlic powder, and white pepper)
  • 1 scotch bonnet, left whole and pierced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp palm oil or vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp miso or ground crayfish substitute (optional)
  • 100 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 100 g spinach or ugu (pumpkin leaves), shredded
  • 1 stock cube (vegan)
  • Scent leaf or basil, torn, to finish

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a pot and fry the onion for 3 minutes; add the pepper-soup spice and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  2. Pour in the stock, add the scotch bonnet, stock cube, and miso if using; bring to a simmer.
  3. Add the mushrooms and simmer for 5 minutes to flavour the broth.
  4. Add the noodles and cook per pack instructions, about 3 minutes, until springy.
  5. Stir in the spinach or ugu and cook for 1–2 minutes until wilted.
  6. Remove the scotch bonnet, taste for salt and heat, and finish with torn scent leaf. Serve piping hot.
