How to Make Vegan Sweet Potato & Black Bean Enchiladas

Saucy rolled tortillas baked under a rich red chilli sauce.

MexicanWestern11 ingredients5 stepsAllergens: gluten

Ingredients

  • 2 sweet potatoes, cubed and roasted
  • 400 g tin black beans, drained
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 8 corn or flour tortillas
  • 2 x 400 g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 100 g vegan cheese, grated
  • Coriander and avocado, to serve

Method

  1. Simmer the onion, garlic, chilli powder, and tomatoes for 15 minutes; blend smooth and season for the sauce.
  2. Mash the roasted sweet potato with the beans, cumin, paprika, and salt.
  3. Spread a ladle of sauce in a baking dish.
  4. Fill and roll the tortillas with the sweet potato mixture; pack them seam-side down in the dish.
  5. Cover with the remaining sauce and the cheese; bake at 190 C (fan 170 C) for 25 minutes. Serve with coriander and avocado.
